# Shared oncology portal — 23 October 2026

The home page now places the twelve HPB oncology roadmaps on the left and the
seven standard/guidance-aligned liver transplant pathways on the right. Existing
HPB URLs are unchanged. Transplant pathways live under `transplant/`.

The transplant export preserves the October 17 clinical content: 46 decisions,
45 step-linked meeting questions, and 19 evidence sources. It includes the seven
V3 protocol DOCX files and fourteen referenced alignment/decision DOCX files,
copied byte-for-byte. Source-version dates remain visible; publication is not a
new clinical review. `transplant/export-manifest.json` records document hashes.

Browser QA also corrected an existing navigation collision: when a meeting
question and a pathway step share an anchor name, `/alignment/` now opens the
question's source comparison rather than jumping back into the pathway step.

Licensed journal PDFs were not published. Evidence links now resolve to official
policy/guidance or publisher/DOI pages and may require institutional access.
High-risk/experimental transplant protocols are not part of this export.

Rebuild from the maintained transplant site's existing `dist` directory:

```sh
python3 tools/build_portal.py /absolute/path/to/standard_protocol_website/dist
python3 tools/validate_portal.py /absolute/path/to/standard_protocol_website/dist
```

The exporter changes hosting/access URLs only. `assets/portal-navigation.js`
provides shared-home and paired tumor/transplant links without changing clinical
content in the twelve existing oncology roadmaps.
