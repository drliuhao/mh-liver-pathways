Hepatocellular adenoma

  • Not applicable. Not applicable. HCA is benign; extrahepatic disease arises only after malignant transformation (about 3–4% in pooled series, 3.1% series-only), when the HCC route and HCC-B5 apply. The HCA pathway already requires no extrahepatic metastasis and no macrovascular invasion where transformation is confirmed.

Pancreatic cysts

  • Not applicable. Not applicable. Cystic lesions are premalignant; an invasive carcinoma with metastases is managed on the PDAC roadmap (PDAC-R1).

Incidental liver lesions

  • Not applicable. Different question. A previously treated extrahepatic cancer is already a risk factor in this roadmap: an indeterminate liver lesion in such a patient is worked up with MRI as a possible metastasis and routed to the matching tumor pathway.
