What changed in the pCCA pathway

Evidence review updated 23 October 2026

Working draft for multidisciplinary discussion. Not ratified institutional clinical policy.

  • Before transplant: OPTN requires an approved neoadjuvant protocol and operative staging but does not mandate a particular radiation technique. Regimen selection remains a program decision.
  • Outside transplant: NCCN lists chemoradiation without requiring prior systemic control. BSG supports selected SBRT/PBT after systemic therapy. A routine survival advantage remains unproven.
  • After resection: NCCN lists chemoradiation for R1 margins or positive nodes and as a nonpreferred R0/node-negative option. Systemic therapy and clinical trials are preferred.
  • Different thresholds: NCCN clinical LT consideration uses ≤3 cm; OPTN allocation requires a single mass <3 cm when present. Source: NCCN v1.2026, original PDF pages 23–24; OPTN 9.5.A.
  • Biliary interventions: Recent RFA trials did not demonstrate routine survival or patency benefit. PDT is not recommended outside trials. I-125 stent results do not establish HAIC efficacy or transplant bridging.

Added evidence

  • Welling, Liver Transplantation 2014: 12 RT-treated patients, 11 receiving SBRT, six transplanted.
  • Soliman, Transplantation Direct 2025: chemotherapy-only versus RT-containing protocols; no significant difference, not proof of equivalence.
  • Hoogwater, BJS Open 2023: lower recurrence hazard but more vascular complications with neoadjuvant chemoradiation; no significant OS difference.
  • ABC-07 planning QA, STRONG II protocol, and STRONG II benchmark QA: planning and research context, not new efficacy results.
