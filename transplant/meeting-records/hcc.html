OPTN 9.5.I · full text
NLRB guidance

NLRB · HCC

Guidance permits selected review after downstaging beyond standardized criteria. For previously treated rupture or primary portal-vein branch invasion, it describes a prolonged stable interval of at least 12 months before some cases may be considered.

Request individual NLRB review when standardized HCC MELD-exception criteria are not met. Document pretreatment tumor burden, rupture or vascular invasion, treatment response, AFP and the observation period.

Pretreatment tumor burden and prior rupture or vascular invasion still determine whether an HCC MELD-exception request needs individual review.

NLRB · HCC · full text
Clinical guidance

AASLD · HCC

“discontinuation of these agents at least 3 months prior to LT”

Short verbatim excerpt · ICI discussion, PDF p.20

AASLD · HCC · full text
Proposed local approach

Explicitly discuss the AASLD 3-month recommendation, agent/last dose, rejection risk and urgency. Any departure needs a documented multidisciplinary rationale; no washout guarantees safety.

Rationale

Eligibility for HCC MELD-exception points and the risk of rejection after checkpoint-inhibitor exposure require separate assessments.

Proposed local approach
