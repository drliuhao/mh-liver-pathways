Proposed local approach

Use intraductal diagnostic approaches; require explicit target/needle-path review for EUS. Nodal sampling is not interchangeable with sampling the primary.

Why this matters

Clinical text and allocation policy are not identical at the boundary.

OPTN 9.5.A

within 30 days prior to the submission of the extension request

Extension imaging · Source

Proposed local approach

Separate initial-request imaging, extension imaging and mandatory pretransplant operative staging; assign a date/owner to each.

Why this matters

Negative pretherapy nodal sampling does not replace posttherapy operative staging.
