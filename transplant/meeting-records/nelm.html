Proposed local approach

Document prior-site clearance and observation separately from current staging. Active nodes stop the route; cleared nodes may be reconsidered under the guidance, unlike the stated lung/bone exclusion.

Rationale

“Any past node is an absolute contraindication” and “nodes do not matter” are both inaccurate.
