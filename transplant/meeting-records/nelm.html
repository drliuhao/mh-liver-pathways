Can prior nodal disease be distinguished from active or solid-organ metastasis?

Pending multidisciplinary approval. Open this question in the pathway →

lymph-nodal Ga68 positive locations

Example of extrahepatic progression · Source

NLRB · NET

zeroed and remained so for at least 6 months

Reconsideration wording · Source

NLRB · NET

Presence of extra-hepatic solid organ metastases (i.e. lungs, bones) should be a permanent exclusion.

NET exclusions · Source

Proposed local approach

Document treatment of previously involved nodes and the recurrence-free interval separately from current staging. Active nodal disease prevents transplant listing under this approach; treated nodes may be reconsidered under NLRB guidance. Lung or bone metastases remain a separate exclusion.

Why this matters

Prior nodal disease that has been removed is assessed differently from active nodal metastasis when considering NET transplantation.

Proposed responsible team: NET MDT · nuclear medicine · transplant surgery
