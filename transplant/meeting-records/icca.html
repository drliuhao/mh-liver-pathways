Which event starts or restarts the six-month stability interval?

Pending multidisciplinary approval. Open this question in the pathway →

NLRB · iCCA

6 months from time of diagnosis or last treatment of tumor stability

iCCA guidance · Source

Very-early iCCA

The very-early cohort reports transplant outcomes; the NLRB guidance specifies the observation interval for MELD-exception review.

· Source

Proposed local approach

Record both diagnosis and last-treatment dates. If the starting point is uncertain, obtain an interpretation from the clinician responsible for MELD-exception requests before submission.

Why this matters

Do not substitute the shortest convenient interval for ambiguous wording.

Proposed responsible team: Coordinator · transplant physician
