iCCA · Check clinical selection criteria — recording template

Does the patient fit the current guidance population and lack a curative resection option?

Download MarkdownResearch data kit · ZIP

Use in the EHR or an approved clinical system. This public page provides a blank template and accepts no patient entries. Record applicable information; the template does not order tests or determine eligibility. Field definitions and repeatable logs are local proposals for clinical documentation and future registry design.

For each value, retain its clinical date, source report / section and reviewer. Use recorded, pending, not done, not applicable, unknown, or not documented; blank does not mean normal or negative. Dates: YYYY-MM-DD, with precision stated for partial dates. Preserve earlier assessments and stable lesion / treatment / specimen IDs.

Sections in this template
  • Assessment identification
  • iCCA / mixed-tumor focality and histology detail
  • Liver lesion map — one row per lesion at each imaging time point
  • Disease extent and staging assessment
  • Resection and alternative-treatment assessment
  • Clinical decision, actions and sign-off
  • Research and data-quality extension

Information checklist for this decision

  1. Multiple nodules: terminology and eligibilityMultifocal means two or more separate tumor nodules; satellite nodules are nearby separate nodules, with no universal iCCA distance definition. Intrahepatic metastases describe spread within the liver. These terms overlap; imaging distribution alone does not establish clonality. An additional indeterminate nodule may be HCC or benign, especially in cirrhosis. Pause the eligibility decision and clarify the diagnosis; consider biopsy when it would change management. Separate HCC alongside iCCA requires explicit program/NLRB interpretation, not assumed eligibility or exclusion.
