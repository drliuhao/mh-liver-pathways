Information checklist for this decision (4 items)

  1. Imaging and lesion map

    Multiphase liver CT or MRI; transplant-radiology review of adequacy and at-risk liver context.

  2. Imaging and lesion map

    For every lesion: segment, maximum diameter, LI-RADS category, imaging date, prior treatment and pretreatment appearance.

  3. Diagnostic context

    AFP with trend; liver disease history. AFP alone does not establish HCC.

  4. Diagnostic context

    If imaging is indeterminate or the liver is outside the imaging-diagnosis population, decide whether tissue is needed.
