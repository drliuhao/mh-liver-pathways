Information checklist for this decision (6 items)

  1. Core milestones

    R0 primary resection; ≥12 months from CRLM diagnosis to initial request.

  2. Core milestones

    Colonoscopy within 12 months before the initial request, with no local recurrence.

  3. Synchronous presentation — additional wording

    Primary resection >6 months after initial diagnosis.

  4. Synchronous presentation — additional wording

    At least 6 months of chemotherapy after primary resection before request.

  5. Synchronous presentation — additional wording

    At least 12 months total stability after initial diagnosis.

  6. Synchronous presentation — additional wording

    Record the actual sequence. Do not delay appropriate primary treatment just to fit a transplant timeline; seek interpretation for a different sequence.
