Information checklist for this decision (4 items)

  1. Liver reserve

    Bilirubin, albumin, INR, creatinine, sodium, platelets; Child-Pugh and MELD assessment.

  2. Liver reserve

    Ascites, encephalopathy, varices, splenomegaly and other portal-hypertension evidence. Assess ECOG, nutrition and frailty.

  3. Tumor stage and operation feasibility

    Chest CT for initial exception staging; liver CT/MRI for vascular invasion and extrahepatic spread.

  4. Tumor stage and operation feasibility

    HPB review: achievable clearance, future liver remnant, inflow/outflow and biliary drainage; record why resection is or is not appropriate.
