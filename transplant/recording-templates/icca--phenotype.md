## Information checklist

- [ ] Multiple nodules: terminology and eligibility: Multifocal means two or more separate tumor nodules; satellite nodules are nearby separate nodules, with no universal iCCA distance definition. Intrahepatic metastases describe spread within the liver. These terms overlap; imaging distribution alone does not establish clonality. An additional indeterminate nodule may be HCC or benign, especially in cirrhosis. Pause the eligibility decision and clarify the diagnosis; consider biopsy when it would change management. Separate HCC alongside iCCA requires explicit program/NLRB interpretation, not assumed eligibility or exclusion.
