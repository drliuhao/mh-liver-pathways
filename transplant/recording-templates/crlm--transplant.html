CRLM · Transplant and follow-up — recording template

Does the benefit-risk decision still hold at transplant, and who follows recurrence?

Download MarkdownResearch data kit · ZIP

Use in the EHR or an approved clinical system. This public page provides a blank template and accepts no patient entries. Record applicable information; the template does not order tests or determine eligibility. Field definitions and repeatable logs are local proposals for clinical documentation and future registry design.

For each value, retain its clinical date, source report / section and reviewer. Use recorded, pending, not done, not applicable, unknown, or not documented; blank does not mean normal or negative. Dates: YYYY-MM-DD, with precision stated for partial dates. Preserve earlier assessments and stable lesion / treatment / specimen IDs.

  • Assessment identification
  • Disease extent and staging assessment
  • Resection and alternative-treatment assessment
  • Clinical transplant evaluation and readiness

  • Post-transplant / program outcomes — repeat at each follow-up
  • Program milestones and follow-up — repeat as events occur

  • Research and data-quality extension
