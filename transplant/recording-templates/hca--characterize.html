Information checklist for this decision (3 items)

  1. Imaging and context

    Contrast MRI: number, largest diameter, growth, exophytic anatomy, hemorrhage and suspected malignant transformation.

  2. Imaging and context

    Record sex, estrogen/androgen exposure, metabolic factors, GSD or Abernethy malformation.

  3. Subtype confirmation

    If subtype changes management, obtain appropriate expert pathology/molecular review. Imaging cannot reliably identify β-catenin activation or distinguish it from HCC.
