Information checklist for this decision (3 items)

  1. Dated clearance evidence

    Primary and any permitted extrahepatic disease resected with no recurrence for ≥6 months before request.

  2. Dated clearance evidence

    Include negative metastatic workup with functional imaging. Guidance says “at least 3 months prior” to initial/extension request; clarify the chronology and image currency with allocation leadership.

  3. Dated clearance evidence

    Document stability/response and liver-volume burden. Milan-NET selection used <50% burden and ≥6 months control; these are not identical to the current NLRB list.
