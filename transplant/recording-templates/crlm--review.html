Information checklist for this decision (2 items)

  1. Review packet

    Include histology/molecular results, primary R0 report, unresectability assessment, baseline tumor burden, all required time intervals and current imaging/CEA. Guidance supports MMaT−20, with floor 15.

  2. Extension evidence

    Every 3 months: CT/MRI chest/abdomen/pelvis and CEA; no hepatic progression or extrahepatic disease, and CEA <80 µg/L.
