  1. Timed imaging

    CT/MRI chest, abdomen and pelvis AND PET within 1 month of the initial request. PET is specified after ≥4 weeks off chemotherapy. Coordinate any treatment pause with oncology.

    Reconcile PET findings with anatomic imaging, CEA and the primary-site record. ctDNA does not replace these required tests.
