An additional indeterminate nodule may be HCC or benign, especially in cirrhosis. Pause the eligibility decision and clarify the diagnosis; consider biopsy when it would change management. Separate HCC alongside iCCA requires explicit program/NLRB interpretation, not assumed eligibility or exclusion.
