Information checklist for this decision (3 items)

  1. Primary-stricture workup

    ERCP cholangiography with brush cytology, FISH/aneuploidy assessment and intraductal biopsy as clinically appropriate; plan drainage with the treating team.

  2. Primary-stricture workup

    Interpret CA 19-9 after addressing cholangitis/obstruction; >100 U/mL is a policy diagnostic alternative only in the absence of cholangitis.

  3. Nodal staging

    Consider EUS sampling of suspicious regional lymph nodes without passing through the primary tumor. Record exactly what was sampled and the needle path.
