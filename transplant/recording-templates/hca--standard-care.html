Information checklist for this decision (3 items)

  1. Conventional management

    Review stopping exogenous hormonal exposure and weight management when relevant. In women, reassess after 6 months of lifestyle modification.

  2. Conventional management

    Resection is recommended in men and proven β-catenin-mutated HCA irrespective of size; in women, resect ≥5 cm or continuing growth after observation when appropriate.

  3. Conventional management

    Hemodynamic instability from bleeding requires urgent stabilization and embolization assessment; residual viable lesion after bleeding may require resection.
