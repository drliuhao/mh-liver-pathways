29 October 2026

  • Transplant pathway pages now end like the roadmaps, with four sections opened by Show: practice choices with limited evidence (64 rules the standard and high-risk protocols already apply that rest on expert opinion, local convention, extrapolation or non-comparative data, each with its basis, what to track and where the protocol states it; extracted from the V16 protocols and checked independently), questions for consensus (an index of every meeting question and high-risk review, each opening where it is set out), team responsibilities and quality monitoring (decision owners and timing, the protocol team, and the quality measures by setting), and the evidence library. Inside the pages, cancer roadmaps are called roadmaps and transplant pathways pathways; the Word files are renamed to match (for example HCC_1_Roadmap.docx, HCC_3_Standard_Pathway.docx, HCC_5_High_Risk_Pathway.docx). The shared top bar is now flush with the page edge, its items are vertically centred and its edges line up with each page’s content. No criterion, recommendation or routing changed. Analyst B.
  • Read in full the papers that were missing from the evidence depots (38 papers filed; 23 more PDFs matched in the evidence libraries, now 596 of 618) and corrected what the roadmaps, treatment matrices and study lines said about them: numbers, study designs, populations and provenance notes. Examples: the ampullary neuroendocrine series reported R0 in 3 of 4 transduodenal cases with a margin recorded (not 75% of 6) and nodes examined in only 1 of 6; the 2018 mixed-tumour transplant series gives 42% against 61% recurrence-free survival in its results but 28% in its abstract; SWOG S0809, Ng 2017, Elfrink 2021, Wei 2025 and the 2013 European cyst consensus are now cited as read. Liver-directed matrix references show their authors and journals; the systemic matrix References view marks the new PDFs. Every change is recorded as an exact text edit in tools/clinical_revisions/2026-10-29-full-text-batch.json; no criterion, threshold, recommendation or routing changed. The CheckMate 8HW first-line figure (54.1 versus 5.9 months, HR 0.21) is left unchanged pending a team decision on which report to cite. Analyst B.
  • Standardized documents: each tumor now has a full-content Word document and a questions-for-consensus Word document for each of its settings (cancer roadmap, standard transplant pathway, high-risk transplant pathway) and one decision-discussion document with every position that differs between papers or guidelines, the proposed selection and space for the group’s decision. Each page offers its files once, from a Documents menu at the top right. Roadmaps show their decisions for consensus in a Questions for consensus section; the extrahepatic-disease page is now an index to where each question is discussed. The treatment-interval, tumor-marker trend and cytoreduction calculators were removed, and the hepatic arterial infusion guide is no longer a standalone resource. Analyst B.
  • Added proposed positions on previously treated extrahepatic disease: one question per tumor in the transplant pathways (HCC-B5, iCCA-B4, pCCA-B1, CRLM-B5, NET-B4, HEHE-B2) and the cancer roadmaps (HCC, combined HCC–CCA, iCCA, hilar cholangiocarcinoma, CRLM, NELM, pNET, PDAC, ampullary), gathered on a new positions page with rules, study results, read status (full text or abstract only) and PubMed, journal and TMC library links. The transplant high-risk reviews carry the revised positions and 11 new sources; each roadmap decision where the question arises links to its position. Where a transplant pathway already asks about extrahepatic disease, the answer now opens the matching review; HCC adds a previously treated extrahepatic metastasis history and iCCA asks about previously proven nodal or extrahepatic disease, both leading to closed outcomes. All positions are OPEN proposals; no standard criterion, allocation rule or roadmap recommendation changed. Abstract-only sources will be re-read in full and the positions revised. Analyst B.
  • Added decision-specific resource tools to the Tools & resources column (the Tools tab on phones) across all 12 roadmaps and seven transplant pathways, each at the decisions it supports and inside the existing Scores & classifications section. New calculators: RECIST 1.1 and mRECIST response, RETREAT, the French AFP model, Metroticket 2.0, a treatment-interval calculator, tumor-marker trend with doubling or halving time, and NELM liver involvement and cytoreduction. New reference tables: MELD exception scores by diagnosis (the page’s own diagnosis highlighted), AFP rules for the HCC exception, clinically significant portal hypertension (Baveno VII), ISGLS bile leak and bleeding grades, NETPET grade, and liver-directed treatment feasibility from the shared matrix. Each roadmap’s own classification and criteria tables (for example Bismuth–Corlette, CRLM unresectability axes, Milan-NET, HCA subtypes and surveillance) are now also offered at the related transplant decisions, loaded from the roadmap rather than copied. Liver volume and portal vein embolization growth link to LiverVolume.com; the Liver Frailty Index and ACS NSQIP open their original calculators. Tools store nothing and do not change recommendations, criteria or routing. Analyst B implementation of Analyst A’s approved inventory.
