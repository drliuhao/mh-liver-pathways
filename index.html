Twelve hepato-pancreato-biliary system roadmaps as working consoles. Each one answers a stage or gate with the standard, the reasonable alternative and what to avoid, with the grade and the paper behind every claim — and says plainly where the evidence does not reach.

What this is, and what it is not

These pages are drafts under active development, prepared for multidisciplinary and institutional governance review. They are not ratified clinical policy, they have not been approved by the specialties named in them, and they must not be used for patient care decisions.

Content is a decision-focused evidence synthesis, not a systematic review. Every criterion drawn from OPTN policy, drug labelling, NCCN or device authorisation must be re-verified against the current source before any use. Clinical transplant candidacy and allocation eligibility are separate judgements.
