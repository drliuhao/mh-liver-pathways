Working material for multidisciplinary review

These roadmaps and protocols are discussion drafts, not approved clinical policy. Source authority, evidence strength and local proposals are distinct. Recheck current allocation policy, guidance and drug/device requirements before clinical use.

Journal sources open on publisher, DOI or official full-text pages; institutional access may be required. Licensed PDFs remain in the local reference depots. High-risk / experimental transplant protocols are being reviewed separately and are not included in the standard-risk column.
