  1. See the tests and information to obtain.
  2. Use the findings to proceed, obtain more information, or select another treatment pathway.
  3. Open the supporting source beside the criterion.
  4. Review orange-labeled high-risk protocols at the relevant decision, including the evidence, limits and approval requirements.

Working material for multidisciplinary review

These roadmaps and protocols are discussion drafts, not approved clinical policy. Source authority, evidence strength and local proposals are distinct. Recheck current allocation policy, guidance and drug/device requirements before clinical use.

Journal sources open on publisher, DOI, TMC Library or official full-text pages; institutional access may be required. Licensed PDFs remain in the local reference depots. Orange-labeled sections integrate proposed high-risk protocols and their limits. Author-created V7 protocol PDFs are available; publication does not activate a transplant protocol or study.
